GUARDRAILS & AGENT WORKFLOW

How this research board works

Transparent evidence beats an impressive-sounding unsupported AI prediction.

1. Confirm the exact market

Player, date, side, line, book, American price, and quote time. A line of 24.5 cannot be compared with 25.5 as though it were identical.

2. Verify the stats

Calculate from real game logs. Exclude DNP. Check sample size and whether the same minutes/role applies today.

3. Check game-day uncertainty

Use the NBA official injury report. Confirm lineup implications close to tip-off; stale odds become research-incomplete.

4. Challenge the thesis

Assign independent agents to stats, injury/rotation, matchup, market price, and counterargument. Log evidence with links and times.

5. Distinguish price from probability

American odds imply a bookmaker break-even rate including vig. Historical hit rate alone cannot establish expected value or an edge.

6. Track predictions honestly

Store pregame snapshots before comparing with outcomes; no hindsight edits. Maintain historical accuracy and calibration only after a meaningful sample.

Multi-agent handoff

Export the board as JSON and give it to the next agent together with AGENT-HANDOFF.md. Each agent should append a source-linked review, never overwrite an earlier review or silently upgrade unknown evidence to verified. The Hub stores the project link and next action; detailed NBA research stays in this app.

This version is a local prototype: no live sportsbook or stat API, cloud synchronization, automatic agent access, or real-money bet execution.
